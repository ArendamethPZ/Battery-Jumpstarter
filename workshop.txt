title=Battery Jumpstarter
description=[h1]Description[/h1]
description=Forgot your car lights on and now your battery is dead? Worry no more! The brand new Stanley Portable Jumpstarter will recharge your battery up to 15% and save the day!
description=
description=[h1]Q&A[/h1]
description=[list]
description=    [*][b]How does the battery jumpstarter work?[/b]
description=    The item can be used only on car batteries that are below 10%. It has a 75% chance of putting the battery at 15%. The item is a drainable that has 5 uses. After all of the uses are depleted, you'll need to recharge it at a generator to use it again. It'll consume one unit of fuel per one recharge. That means to fully recharge the item, it'll consume 5 fuel units. The item is portable and doesn't need you to be next to a generator to use it.
description=
description=    [*][b]Where can I find the jumpstarter?[/b]
description=    The item may spawn in mechanic shops, car supply stores, garages, gigamarts, and vehicles trunks.
description=
description=    [*][b]How do I use the battery jumpstarter?[/b]
description=    Go to your vehicle mechanics window, right click on the battery, and click [b]"Jumpstart"[/b]
description=
description=    [*][b]How do I recharge the battery jumpstarter?[/b]
description=    Go to a generator. Ensure that its currently turned on, then right click it and click [b]"Recharge Jumpstarter"[/b]
description=[/list]
